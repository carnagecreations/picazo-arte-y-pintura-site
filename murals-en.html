Murals & Commissions

From bare wall to finished piece

Every commission starts as a conversation and ends with a wall (or a canvas) nobody walks past without looking twice.

Large-Scale Murals

Exteriors, interiors, full building wraps. Restaurants, storefronts, community walls — painted on-site, built to hold up outdoors.

Pet & Character Portraits

A beloved pet alongside its owner, a custom character brought to life, a portrait painted on a wall that's just yours.

Canvas & Custom Art

Original canvas pieces for a home or office, sized and styled to the space and the story you want it to tell.

How It Works

The process, wall to wall

Consultation

We talk through the space, the budget, and what you want the piece to say — in person or over the phone.

Concept & Sketch

You get a sketch or color concept before any paint touches the wall, so there are no surprises.

Wall Prep

Surface is cleaned, primed, and measured out so the piece fits the space exactly as planned.

Painting Days

The mural goes up in stages — you're welcome to stop by and watch it come together.

Reveal & Photos

Final walkthrough, touch-ups if needed, and a full set of photos for your own use.

Ready to talk about your wall?

Bring a photo of the space and a rough idea — that's plenty to start with.
