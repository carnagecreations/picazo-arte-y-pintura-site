Picazo — Visual Artist

Painting Yuma, Somerton, and the border region, one wall and one canvas at a time.

In Her Words

Why I paint

“I love art, and I firmly believe that everything visual around you can influence you — for better or worse. We owe it to everyone we want to reach to express love, joy, and hope visually.”

That belief shows up in every wall Picazo takes on — whether it's a full building exterior, a tropical scene for a restaurant patio, or a single portrait of somebody's dog. Contemporary work on canvas, customized murals, and everything in between, always hand-painted, always on-site.
